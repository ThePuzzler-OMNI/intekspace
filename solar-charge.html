Twin extremes placement

Shared charge bank sits toward community density (one instrument, many kits). Weather, chemistry, and field honesty sit on the farmer pole. The middle story — “just plug into the wall and forget” — is already crowded.

Same refused lists: measure the breathe of energy in and out; name cloudy-day limits.

The need in the room

Why this educates — not what ships in a box.

Electronics kits need power. A room of students on play day wants packs that are fresh enough, of assorted types, without treating stored energy as a junk drawer.

The sun already provides. Prefer panel input over default grid story. Keep an account over time: what is full, what is aging, what is quarantined, what failed last cycle. Health is a trend — not a single green LED.

Home Assistant (local-friendly) is an interconnect surface to monitor panel metrics and bank/pack status — not the product, not a cloud trust root by default. Same instinct as house-scale continuity elsewhere: local first, cloud secondary.

What this is — and is not

Is

  • Education objective under One Mission
  • Category of science: solar path → typed charge bank → kit loads
  • Classroom / house need: play-day packs + status and health ledger
  • Local monitoring path (e.g. Home Assistant) for panel and bank over time
  • Evidence shape: typed packs, account snapshot, health trend, one limitation named
  • Sibling to Sun → water & charge and Electrons & code

Is not · refused

  • A promised kit BOM or charge-station SKU on this page
  • Free-energy or “grid is obsolete” marketing
  • Junk-drawer charging of mixed chemistries
  • Unsupervised roof work or toy treatment of stored energy
  • Cloud Home Assistant as the trust root by default
  • A substitute for OMNIbot or OMNI Home

Evidence and safety

Evidence (when you Apply and build): panel/input path named; storage and load path named; status ledger for packs; one health trend or quarantine decision; one limitation named on camera (weather, chemistry mismatch, or degraded cell).

Guardian expected for outdoor panel steps and any mains fallback. Mixed chemistries need typed bays and correct charge profiles — no casual mixing.

Implementation videos of real attempts and critique will be the example detail when they land. Until then this page holds the need, the category, and the refusals.
