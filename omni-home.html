The Vaults

Plural on purpose. Primary is local. Cloud is not the trust root.

The Vaults are the primary store of the house record — local, plural, held under stewardship rather than platform ownership of the source.

There is a duplicate path that looks like cloud vaults: the rich and wasteful man’s solution, because we cannot trust cloud… yet. That duplicate is secondary and provisional. It is not where trust roots. We do not claim cloud is solved. We claim the family record should not depend on it as the only ground.

From that ground there is a humble offer to join OMNI — One Mission as a living door, not a product SKU. In humble honest opinion, that path is what the world needs for peace. That is opinion and invitation. It is not a deliverable of OMNI Home hardware or software.

Atticus in the house

Secondary bridge: personal, parental, professional, political (public), and philosophical presence — with a correspondent that stays discrete.

OMNI Home is an Atticus: a reliable, discrete, trustworthy correspondent for half-baked thoughts. It listens to the house without judgments. It logs for the private record.

That record is released upon death of all parties — not streamed, not sold, not performed. Growth and learning occur in the safe vault. Families have problems. The biology layer is in scope. This is where coherence with architecture plans must stay on the record: multi-party consent, definition of “party,” and posthumous release are design constraints, not slogans.

The correspondent layer is architecture-stable. Today that may read as an LLM; tomorrow it may be named differently — including deeper architectural shifts. The role does not change because the model generation name does.

Trust is earned in architecture and refusal — not in “omg must build trust” theater.
