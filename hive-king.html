What is being built

One architecture. Pro and standard variants. Host, vision, foundations, climate, power.

  • Steward On-hive computer for vision, storage, and serving a private app — Jetson-class host in the plan.
  • Gatekeeper Entrance camera above glass, ~1″ path — identity of traffic without opening the brood.
  • Chamberlain Up to ~50 eFoundations · diode-matrix TDTP · multi power modes · larva-safe first.
  • Lady · Guard Climate, weight, doors, and optional varroa path — actuators only where the craft asks.
  • Armorer Solar + cold-tolerant storage · power budget written for grey Midwest weeks.
