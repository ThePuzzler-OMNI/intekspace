The Five P's of Freedom

Personal, Parental, Professional, Public, and Pastoral.

Personal is yourself. Parental is your offspring only — a mate or spouse is separate. Professional is your craft and the people you associate with for a common purpose. Public is who influences and shapes your environment — the outward civic leg, formerly called Political. Pastoral is how you affect your environment: the inner, shepherding, giving side that none of the other four touch.

This is a member’s One Mission page: a guiding light in your own words, then goals and objectives you set and keep. Biology first. The page is a tool under stewardship — tool not throne. OMNI may help you write. It does not set the aim, rank the lanes, or hold the archive. The archive stays with you.

One Mission

The guiding light is yours. This site does not supply it. Write one to three sentences in your own words — what you are steering by this season — then give each lane a direction and a few steps you can check.

Goal
The direction, in plain words. Stable for a season, such as a year.
Objective
A concrete, checkable step toward the goal. One to three per goal, each with a “done looks like” line and a target date.
Door
Which sister site helps with that lane. The door is a workshop. You remain the steward.
Steward check
One honest question at review: does this still serve life — body, home, neighbor?

Five lanes

Hold the lanes together, the way the Forge holds Philosophy last as the integrator. No single lane runs off alone. Examples below are samples, not a member’s mission.

Highlighted from your link

Personal

Yourself

Yourself — body, mind, soul, and the craft of your own life. A direction for health, character, and devotion. An objective looks like a habit or skill with a visible check.

Example · not a member

Goal. Keep my body strong enough to work the land at 60.

Objective. Walk 30 minutes, 5 days a week, through end of quarter — done looks like 60 logged walks.

Goal. Grow in daily devotion.

Objective. Morning quiet time before screens, 6 days a week for 8 weeks.

Door: One Mission Network — mission, community, and Forge Lab. Reflection: Soul-time Mirror.

Parental · offspring only

Raising and teaching your offspring

Your offspring only. A mate or spouse is a separate bond, not this lane. What the parent wants to cultivate with the children: curiosity, craft, faith, kindness. An objective looks like a shared, parent-led learning activity.

Interest and learning only. The parent writes and holds this lane. No child accounts, no child data, and no pay or job language for children.

Example · not a member

Goal. Grow a love of how things work at home.

Objective. One build-together afternoon per month this season (for example a small garden bed), parent-led — done looks like three finished builds.

Door: Institute of Mature Imagination — learning and young-builder curiosity. Reading on this site: Intek Education.

Professional

Craft and common purpose

Your craft, and the people you associate with for a common purpose. A product or service that serves others and sustains the household, toward freedom from personal debt — the Forge’s graduation idea. An objective looks like a shipped or sold unit of real work, or a skill proven by evidence. Keep amounts off this page.

Example · not a member

Goal. Offer one honest product that pays its own way.

Objective. List my first item and complete one person-to-person sale by the target date — done looks like one delivered order and one note of feedback.

Door: OMNI Exchange — person-to-person exchange. On Intek Space: Education, store, projects.

Highlighted from your link

Public · outward civic

Who shapes your environment

Who influences and shapes your environment. This is the outward civic leg — formerly called Political. Offices, customs, and the public square that press on the household from outside. An objective looks like a named attention: who sets the terms, written down, and one civic room you enter to listen. The sheet is not a campaign. The civic leg stays.

Example · not a member

Goal. See who shapes the ground I live on.

Objective. Write the local offices and customs that influence this household this season — done looks like one page naming who sets the terms.

Goal. Stand in the civic room as a neighbor.

Objective. Attend one open civic meeting this season to listen — done looks like a note of who spoke and what shapes the street.

Door: One Mission Network — community beside the civic leg. The giving side of this life sits in Pastoral, not here.

Highlighted from your link

Pastoral

How you affect your environment

How you affect your environment. The inner, shepherding, giving side that none of the other four touch. Service, charity, and care given from the inside — shepherding, not an office. An objective looks like a named act of service or giving with a check. Keep amounts off this page.

Example · not a member

Goal. Shepherd the ground I can actually touch.

Objective. Help at one community work day per month for a season — done looks like the season’s work days kept.

Goal. Give with intent.

Objective. Choose one charity cause and set a personal giving rhythm (amount kept private).

Door: One Mission Foundation — charity and giving. Community also lives with the One Mission Network.
