Yard-to-Loop

A conversion instrument for the yard—not a toy kit.

Built under a philosophy on life: make the death-to-life breathe measurable on real land—intake, convert, output, residual—so intelligence and craft expand without hidden extinction cost.

Peer level to Hive King in seriousness (budget, sectors, proof). Hive King remains starship-class apiary R&D; Yard-to-Loop is the yard’s field instrument.

What it is

Core idea

A contained conversion path for yard biomass / insect loops (attract or colony modes, process chamber, two honest outputs). Designed so a household or student team can measure the land-per-life cost of what they grow and convert.

Not “a kit in a box” only

Modules, sensors, safety, sector placement, and multi-week DMAIC are part of the project. Education seats may join as apprentices; the project itself is costed and engineered as a real product path.

Method (non-negotiable)

  • Philosophy first — why this conversion serves life under stewardship.
  • Sector overlays — sun, water, wind, access, neighbor risk, legal before BOM (define).
  • DMAIC — define → measure breathe → analyze sector failures → improve → control pack (full).
  • Twin extremes — community-dense automation vs farmer deep practice; middle is elsewhere (why).
  • Refused lists — no storm-drain fantasy; no human-food claims from uncontrolled residual (control on marketing).

Road (quiet order)

  1. Lock site sector overlay + refused placements for a real yard
  2. MVP module set: intake · process · two outputs · measure points
  3. Phase-1 data sheet: breathe metrics over weather, not only bench day
  4. Student / partner evidence pack (video + graphics under Education Exchange rubric)
  5. Manufacture/assemble path only after measures survive real sectors
