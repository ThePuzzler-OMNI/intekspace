Poop-to-Loop

Residual conversion under hard refusals—not a joke name, not a toy kit.

Same spine as Yard-to-Loop and the same philosophy: consume death to breed life under stewardship, with the breathe measured where residual actually lives—odor, seal, water, legal, neighbor.

Peer level in seriousness to Yard-to-Loop and to Hive King (cost, engineering, multi-phase proof). Higher sector risk surface than yard biomass; marketing cannot override safety.

What it is

Core idea

A conversion instrument for residual organic streams that refuses industrial anonymity: intention, limit, measurement, and honest residual—not “away” down a drain.

Substantial project

Enclosures, seals, process stages, sensors, and control packs are real BOM and real labor. Education partners may apprentice; the project is not reduced to a classroom handout.

Refused lists (control on marketing)

  • No storm-drain or “it just goes away” claims
  • No human-food claims from uncontrolled residual
  • No scale-up until measures survive real sectors (weather, odor, access, legal)
  • No hiding residual mass or energy cost in the story

See refused lists in the glossary.

Method

Philosophy → sector overlays → DMAIC → twin extremes deploy paths. Sector failures (splash, odor path to neighbor) are first-class failures—not “later.”

Road (quiet order)

  1. Define residual class + legal/access map + absolute refusals
  2. MVP process train with seal / odor / mass balance measure points
  3. Phase-1 field log under weather and household practice
  4. Partner / education evidence only after Phase-1 honesty holds
  5. Manufacture path only when control pack is teachable and repeatable
