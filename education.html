Education Exchange

Kits and project seats.
Evidence of work. Honest energy.

All great works begin with a philosophy on life. Education here follows that order: meaning → constraints → build → evidence—not gadget-first novelty.

Provide a kit or project seat + protocol + mentor/Grok path. Require video and graphic evidence of material use, function, energy conversion, and philosophy alignment (One Mission · honest breathe · mature imagination). Not school bureaucracy as the first door.
