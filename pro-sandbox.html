    5 Export this design

    Why export? Each still design is a small, honest specimen: what a person wanted to see, where it sat, how much of the world it covered, and what the device would have to sense to make it live. Later, with consent on each one, designs like these can help train and specify the Forward View hardware. The person stays the judge of what goes in.

    The JSON holds the card layout, your checklist, and the sensor note. It holds no photo and no location. The PNG is redrawn from scratch, so it carries no camera metadata. Both files save to this device.

    Steward now, members later

    These are plans written down, not offers. There is nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for on this page.

    • Steward · now. The steward uses this sandbox in the open, on his own device. Designs live in the files he downloads.
    • Members · later (planned). Save designs to a member profile. This needs a backend and the steward’s yes before it exists.
    • Builders · later (planned). Share chosen designs with the hardware build, with consent asked for each design.
