Why it sits here

A continuity stack — wearable, mergeable, mobile command — is built from parts someone can see and close. This objective is that literacy: the atomic layer, not the finished presence line.

The need in the room

Why this educates — not what ships in a box.

The modern world already runs on electrons and code. Much of K–12 still treats that as optional garnish. This track is foundational: anyone who walks in — any age — can build the baseline of sensing, control, and a small wireless node under a real need, not a worksheet.

Under One Mission and the primary goal, the point is sharper: grok the smallest components of what a lasting, enduring, space-traveling lifeform needs. Without that literacy, “mergeable” and “command” stay slogans.

What this is — and is not

  • A promised BOM or brand cart on this page

Evidence and safety

Evidence (when you Apply and build): working circuit or node demo; power path explained; one failure found and fixed on camera; in your own words, how this component sits under a lasting / space-capable need — without claiming that lifeform is finished.

Safety is hazard-based, not age-based. Welcome is any age; where a step can shock or burn, name the hazard and use adult presence for that step — without turning the track into an age gate.

Implementation videos of real attempts will be the example detail when they land. Public About copy does not promise a specific kit list.
